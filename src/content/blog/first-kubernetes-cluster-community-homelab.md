Kubernetes has a reputation for being expensive to learn — spin up a managed cluster, forget to tear it down, and your card gets a surprise. The KCTE community homelab fixes that: it's shared hardware, sitting in a rack, free for members to break.

This month we walked a handful of first-timers from "what's a pod?" to a running three-node cluster they deployed an app onto. We used k3s to keep things light, mapped out namespaces so everyone stays in their own lane, and only set one node on fire (figuratively — it just needed a reboot).

If you've been meaning to get hands-on with containers but didn't want the bill, this is your on-ramp. Ping an organizer for homelab access and we'll get you an account and a quickstart.
