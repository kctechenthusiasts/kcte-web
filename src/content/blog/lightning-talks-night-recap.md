Five speakers, five minutes each, zero slides allowed to run long. May's lightning talks night packed more ideas into half an hour than most conferences manage in a day, and the room loved every second of it.

The lineup ranged from a solar-powered Raspberry Pi bird feeder that texts you when a cardinal shows up, to a Rust budgeting app one member built because they "didn't trust the cloud with grocery math." We also got a live SQL-injection demo (on a sandbox, relax), a homegrown RSS reader, and a delightfully cursed Excel-as-a-database talk.

Want to give one next month? You don't need to be an expert — half the fun is watching people demo something they finished last weekend. Sign up in Discord and we'll save you a slot.
