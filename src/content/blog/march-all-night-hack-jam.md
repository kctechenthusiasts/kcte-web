There's a specific kind of clarity that only shows up around 2 a.m., when the pizza is gone, the coffee is questionable, and the only thing left to do is ship. March's all-night hack jam had twelve of us chasing that feeling, and three projects actually crossed the finish line.

The standouts: a Discord bot that turns our event RSVPs into a live attendance graph, a tiny static-site generator someone wrote "to spite Markdown," and a hardware build that blinks an LED matrix in time with the room's noise level. One ambitious AR project did not make it — it caught fire, figuratively, around hour six — but the demo of what *did* work got a standing ovation anyway.

Hack nights aren't about finishing. They're about the company you keep while you try. Bring a half-baked idea to the next one and find out.
