The hardest part of any meetup is the door. You picture a room full of people who already know each other, talking in acronyms you've never heard. Here's the truth: most of us felt exactly that way the first time, and KCTE is built so you don't have to.

A typical night is a short talk or demo, some unstructured hang time, and food or drinks afterward. You don't need a laptop, a project, or a job in tech. You need a name and a willingness to say hi. And if you tell someone you're a beginner, you'll get the opposite of judgment — people love explaining the thing they're excited about.

Come once with zero expectations. Stand near the snacks, ask one person what they're working on, and see how it goes. That's the whole secret.
